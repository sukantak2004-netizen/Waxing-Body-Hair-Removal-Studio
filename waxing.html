After Your Appointment

Aftercare
Tips

The first 48 hours are key to keeping your skin smooth, calm, and beautiful. Follow these simple tips for the best results.

Soothe Hydrate Protect Repeat
Ask Our Team

Avoid Heat for 24 Hours

Skip hot showers, saunas, and intense workouts for the first 24 hours. Heat opens pores and can cause irritation.

Moisturize Daily

Apply a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer after every shower to keep skin soft and prevent ingrown hairs.

Protect from Sun

Waxed skin is more sun-sensitive. Use SPF 30+ on exposed areas and avoid direct sun exposure for 48 hours.

No Tight Clothing

Wear loose, breathable fabrics for the first 24 hours to let your skin breathe and reduce friction.

Exfoliate After 3 Days

Gently exfoliate 2-3 times per week after the first 72 hours to prevent ingrown hairs and keep skin smooth.

Book Every 4-6 Weeks

Regular waxing keeps regrowth fine and soft. We'll send a friendly reminder when it's time for your next visit.
